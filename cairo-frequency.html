About Cairo Frequency

What we do

Cairo Frequency is an independent digital news page covering Egyptian and Arab music and celebrity news — new releases, concerts, festivals and the stories artists break on their own channels.

We monitor the artists we cover every 30 minutes, around the clock. When a song drops or a concert is announced, the story goes out on our Instagram first — artwork carrying the news and the CF mark, with the full details in the caption — and the confirmed stories are collected here.

Our coverage is in English, for Arab music fans everywhere.
